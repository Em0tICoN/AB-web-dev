Article of midevil and renissance weopons

SHORTSWORD

This is the classic one handed sword, usually made of steel or iron. classic sword shape, blade, handle, crossguard. very basic

LONGSWORD

the classic two handed sword. just think of a shortsword but longer blade and handle. somtimes the crossguard can be longer and it can be held one handed, its just not as effective as if your going to be using a shortsword one handed.

DAGGER

think a smaller shortsword. its got a small to no crossguard, and a very short handle and blade.

BROADSWORD

a mix of the longsword and shortsword, kinda the medium beetween them, can be used one or two handed. somtimes actually heavier than the longsword, but thats not too often

RAPIER

a sword for cutting and stabbing. while the londsword, shortsword, and so on can stab two, none of them come close to the stabbing power of the rapier, even though it cant cut as well as the others.

SMALLSWORD

A lot like the rapier but it cannot cut. dull edges but the most fine point, ment for stabbing, and stabbing only.

BATTLE AXE

There is many different variations of the axe but the best way to describe it is the oppsite of the smallsword. only ment for cutting, no stabbing. and this one isnt a sword, its got 1-2 blades on the top half of the axe.

KATANA

this sword is the oppisite of the rapier, for it is mostly made for cutting, and exels at that, but its not very good at the stabs.

ZWEIHANDER

Giant sword. like friggin HUGE. one of my favorite weopons, ment for controlling the situations in 1vX's and can cut and stab. it also is basically a gigantic swiss army knife for only combat situations, incuding:
  • Giant handle for 2 handed weilding.
  • Giant crossguard for protection from other weopons.
  • Giant blade for cleaving through opponents and controlling multpile opponent situations.
  • A parry gaurd, which is a tiny little spiky thing on the blade ment for catching weopons.
  • Somtimes has a wavy blade, which makes swords get caught and slide down the blade awkwardly so the opponets will have a harder time hitting you.


GREATSWORD

a bigger longsword. not the size of the zweihander, but still pretty big. its also really basic, and shouldnt be too interesting. though unlike the longsword, it has to be held 2 handed, while the longsword can be handled with on hand.

POLEHAMMER

this weopon is really diverse and has options for a LOT of situations. if your opponent is in armor, there is a blut part for dealing with armor better. then theres a stabby tip for if you need to get somone farther away or through chainmail, and a hooked part behind the blunt part for stabbing, but with a slicing motion. ultimite anti-armor weopon.

KATAR

a blade for your fist. cant really do to much, as it is just a shortsword if the crossgaurd covered your front fingers near your knuckles, a the blade wasput on the part next to your knuckls. it is not that interesting, but hey, it works.

MAUL

a big hammer. just a big hammer. popularized through video games, and a lot of people think its only from there but we have found real life examples of a maul.

MACE AND FLANGED MACE

a mace is a big stick with a big ball on the end. then there is a flanged mace, which can only be discribed by having a bunch of tiny ax head things on the ball on the end, and the ball is also bigger. just a bludgeon weopon for bonk or bonk with some extra stuff.

MORNING STAR

its a mace, but instead of flangs, this one has random spikes.

FLAIL

a flail is just a morning star but with a chain attached to the head and the handle for a little bit of extra momentum, for less presice strikes.

CRUDGEL

a bat, somtimes metal, somtimes spiked, and somtimes wooden, somtimes has a hammer head. also is usually leaning or tilted in a weird way.
